## What Happens When You Record A Voice Note

```
You open Notes panel
Tap "Record a Note"
Speak in Malayalam:
"ഇന്ന് Dr. Edwina വരുന്നു,
 extension decision എടുക്കണം"

ARYA does this automatically:
  1. Records your voice
  2. Transcribes to Malayalam text
  3. Saves with timestamp
  4. Searchable instantly
```

## Real Use Cases — Doctor Example

```
Use Case 1 — Clinical reminder:
  Walking between patients
  Tap mic, speak:
  "Bed 7 — repeat CBC tomorrow morning,
   discuss with family about discharge"
  
  Saved instantly.
  Check it later between consults.

Use Case 2 — Article idea:
  Reading a paper at night
  Tap mic, speak:
  "Write about POCUS in rural ER —
   how we use it differently from textbook"
  
  Saved. Open later when writing.

Use Case 3 — Administrative:
  Walking to canteen
  Tap mic, speak:
  "Remind about NABH audit documents —
   due next Friday"
  
  Saved. Search "NABH" to find it.

Use Case 4 — Personal:
  Driving home
  Tap mic, speak:
  "Buy Amma's medicine —
   Amlodipine 5mg"
  
  Saved. Never forgotten.
```

## What ARYA Does With Notes — The Powerful Part

```
This is where it becomes special.

In a chat conversation you say:
"What did I want to follow up on?"

ARYA searches your voice notes:
"From your note yesterday at 11:30 PM —
 you wanted to follow up on cultures
 for the MDR patient in bed 4.
 
 Also from this morning — the extension
 decision about Dr. Edwina."

Your voice notes become memory.
ARYA connects them to your conversations.
```
