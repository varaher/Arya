## Fix 1 — Tap Outside to Close (Overlay Dismiss)

Every panel/modal that slides in from the side has a semi-transparent overlay behind it. That overlay needs an `onClick` handler:

```tsx
{/* OVERLAY — tap to close */}
<div
  className="fixed inset-0 bg-black/50 z-40"
  onClick={() => setActivePanel(null)}  // or setShowGoals(false), etc.
/>

{/* PANEL — sits above overlay */}
<div className="fixed right-0 top-0 h-full w-[85%] z-50 bg-white ...">
  {/* panel content */}
</div>
```

**The key:** overlay is `z-40`, panel is `z-50`. Clicking the dark left strip closes everything.

## Fix 2 — Navigation Bar Refinements

```tsx
{/* Bottom Nav — fixed, safe area aware */}
<nav className="fixed bottom-0 left-0 right-0 z-30
                bg-[#1a1a1a] border-t border-white/10
                pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom,0px)]">
  <div className="flex items-center justify-around h-14">
    {navItems.map(item => (
      <button
        key={item.id}
        onClick={() => navigate(item.path)}
        className={`flex flex-col items-center gap-1 px-4 py-2 rounded-xl
                    transition-all duration-200
                    ${isActive(item.path)
                      ? 'text-amber-400'           // active: amber
                      : 'text-white/40'             // inactive: faded
                    }`}
      >
        <item.icon size={20} />
        <span className="text-[10px] font-medium">{item.label}</span>
      </button>
    ))}
  </div>
</nav>

{/* CRITICAL — page content must not hide behind nav */}
<main className="pb-[calc(56px+env(safe-area-inset-bottom,0px))]">
  {/* your page */}
</main>
```
