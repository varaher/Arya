## 🧠 ARYA Core — Your Personal Thinking & Growth Assistant

### Think Through Anything
Bring any decision, dilemma, or problem. ARYA gives you a direct answer first, then depth. It won't hedge everything or ask clarifying questions instead of answering. It thinks with you, not at you.

### Goals & Progress Tracking
Set goals, break them into steps, track progress. ARYA checks in when a goal has gone quiet — curious, not judgmental. Goals 21+ days untouched get a gentle hygiene prompt: Keep it · Pause it · Let it go.

### Memory Across Sessions
ARYA remembers what matters — your goals, your patterns, things you've shared — and weaves them into every conversation naturally. No "I remember you said..." announcements. It just knows.

Not all problems need the same kind of thinking. One tap before any conversation changes how ARYA shows up:

## 🎭 Hard Conversation Rehearsal

Rehearse before any difficult conversation. ARYA plays the other person — your manager, colleague, parent, investor, administrator. Three difficulty levels:

- 🙂 **Realistic** — firm but fair
- 😤 **Tougher** — sharp, direct, asks about liability and cost immediately
- 😠 **Hardest** — already against you; you have to genuinely change their mind

Ends with specific coaching: what landed, what to sharpen, the one thing to carry into the real conversation.

## 📋 Voice Notes & Study Notes

**Voice Notes:** Record thoughts on the go. ARYA transcribes in your language, summarises into bullet points, extracts tasks, names, and deadlines automatically. Save action items directly as goals in one tap.

**Study Notes:** When you're studying or preparing a presentation, ARYA auto-saves structured notes from your conversation — including likely exam questions. Ask for a PPT outline and ARYA gives you the full slide structure, ready to use.

## 📄 Document Intelligence — Upload, Understand, Act

Upload any document — or point your camera at one. ARYA reads it, explains it in plain language, and turns it into action.

**What you can upload:**
PDF · PowerPoint · Word document · Image · Handwritten notes · Printed text in any Indian language

**What ARYA does with it:**

- **Summarises** the document in clear bullet points — no jargon
- **Extracts tasks** found in the document → save as goals in one tap
- **Finds dates and deadlines** → set reminders in one tap
- **Generates exam questions** from study material (NCERT, coaching notes, lecture slides)
- **Pulls out key terms** and definitions
- **Answers your questions** about the document — from the document, not from general knowledge
- **Creates a PPT** from any document or study topic — downloadable .pptx file

**Works in any Indian language.** Upload a Malayalam prescription, a Hindi legal notice, a Tamil medical report. ARYA reads it and explains it in plain English — or in the same language.

**Everything connects:**
Tasks from the document → Goals panel
Dates from the document → Reminders panel
Key points from the document → Notes panel
Questions about the document → Ask ARYA in chat

Like a knowledgeable friend who read it for you, pulled out what matters, and is ready to explain the rest.

## ⚖️ Niti — Business Thinking Partner

A separate dark-mode space built for business decisions and market thinking. Two modes:

### Decisions
Bring your real business situation — a decision you're wrestling with, a plan you want stress-tested, a people situation, or a need to think out loud. ARYA works through it using structured thinking. After each response: one push question to go deeper + three follow-up chips. Every session is saved as a **Thinking Journal** entry — a written record of what you wrestled with and what emerged.

### Market Lens
- Live indicative indices — NIFTY, SENSEX, Bank Nifty, Nifty IT — with sparkline charts
- Rotating news cards with market impact tags — tap "What does this mean for me?" for ARYA's analysis
- **Portfolio Journal** — log your holdings, ARYA asks honest Socratic questions about each one. Never gives buy/sell advice. Helps you think.
- Think with ARYA — 4 pre-loaded market questions + open input

## 📖 Drishya — Stories That Find You

A story experience unlike anything else. You describe what you're looking for — a feeling, a situation, a character, a scene — and ARYA generates a complete story, delivered whole. Drawn from India's 5,000-year tradition: Panchatantra, Jataka tales, Mahabharata, Upanishads, regional folk wisdom — retold for your exact moment.

Three modes:
- **Night Stories** — for the end of a long day
- **Film Scenes** — cinematic, character-driven
- **Everyday** — stories that meet ordinary moments

Stories can be listened to in voice, saved, or simply read. No sources. No citations. Just the story.

## 🌙 KAAL — Cosmic Timing & Cycles

A personal timing companion. Set up once using what you know — your Western star sign, your Vedic Rashi (Moon sign), or just your birth date. Three views:

- **Today** — what kind of day it is energetically, your best window, what to handle with care
- **This Week** — the rhythm of the week ahead, day by day
- **Your Chapter** — the longer arc you're moving through

Includes your Cosmic Profile and Venus Cycle tracking. Used not as prediction, but as a lens for self-awareness and timing decisions. Based on Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Phaladeepika, Saravali, and Thirukkural.

## 💪 Prana — Health Companion

Your personal health coach. Build your profile — height, weight, sex, activity level. Prana tracks what matters:

- BMI and BMR calculated personally — not generic charts
- Heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), steps, stress, calories, weight
- Sleep tracking with 7-day chart
- Blood pressure and blood glucose monitoring

ARYA coaches you based on your actual numbers, not population averages. Asks the questions a good doctor would ask before giving advice. Wearable sync with Apple Health, Google Fit, boAt and Noise — coming soon.

*ARYA is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. For any health concern, see a doctor.*

## 🔒 Privacy & Your Data

Full **DPDP Act 2023** compliance. You are always in control:

- Delete specific categories of data (conversations, goals, health, memory, voice notes)
- Delete everything from a date range
- Full account deletion — permanent, complete, confirmed
- Every deletion is audited — not what was deleted, only that it happened
- Your data is never sold. Never shared with advertisers. Never used to train AI models without your consent.
