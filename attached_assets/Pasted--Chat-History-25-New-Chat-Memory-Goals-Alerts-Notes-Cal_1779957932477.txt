The Priest and the Two Requests

The second said, “Since you have good contact with God, please ask Him to help me—give me money, stability, anything basic that can help.”

When the priest met God and shared these requests, God answered:

To the second person: “Ask him to be grateful for what he already has.”

When the priest relayed this, the first person said, “Why should I stop feeling grateful? He has already given me so much.” The second said, “Why should I feel grateful? He hasn’t given me anything.”
