## Why Summary Matters

```
Without summary:
  Record 2 minute note
  Later: read full transcript
  Scan through everything
  Find what you need
  Time consuming ❌

With summary:
  Record 2 minute note
  Later: see 5 bullet points
  Instantly know what it was about
  Tap to read full if needed
  10 seconds ✅
```

## What ARYA Extracts Automatically

```
From any voice note ARYA identifies:

TASKS → things to do
  "Need to call director"
  "Follow up cultures bed 7"

DECISIONS → things to decide
  "Extension decision today"
  "NABH audit approach"

REMINDERS → time-based
  "Meeting tomorrow 10 AM"
  "Friday deadline"

PEOPLE → who is involved
  "Dr. Edwina, Dr. Neeraj, management"

KEY FACTS → important information
  "Shortage from July"
  "Bed 7 MDR patient"
```
