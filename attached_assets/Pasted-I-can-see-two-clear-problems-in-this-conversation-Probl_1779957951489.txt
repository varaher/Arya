  Sha-ra-va-na-bha-va  (starts with Sha)

  Na-bha-va-sha-ra-va  (starts with Na)
  Bha-va-sha-ra-va-na  (starts with Bha)
  Va-sha-ra-va-na-bha  (starts with Va)

Each rotation is associated with
one of the six faces (Arumugan/Shanmukha)
of Lord Muruga in the Skanda tradition.

This practice is mentioned in some
versions of Skanda Kavacham and
related Shaiva Siddhanta texts.
