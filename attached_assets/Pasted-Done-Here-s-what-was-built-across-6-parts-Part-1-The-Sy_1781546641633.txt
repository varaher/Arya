## Part 1 — The System Prompt

The most important piece. What makes Niti different from every other AI business tool.

The key rules baked in:

```
THE FIRST RESPONSE RULE:
Never give advice in your first response.
Never give a framework.
Ask ONE question.

Not: "What are your options?"
Yes: "Is the audit deadline a real
      constraint — or a reason to delay
      something that feels risky?"

The first question is everything.
It tells them you're paying attention.
It tells them this isn't a form.
```

---

## Part 2 — The Opening Screen

```
What's on your mind?
─────────────────────
Tell Niti what you're working through.
No structure needed — just talk.

[5-line textarea — autofocused]

[🎤 Talk instead]  [Start thinking →]

Jump straight in:
💥 Stress-test an idea
🧭 Stuck on a decision
📈 Think through the market
👥 People situation
🔗 Full analysis

────── or use structured modes ──────
⚡ Quick modes — Decision · Market · People
```

Clean. Open. The textarea is the hero. Everything else is secondary.

---

## Part 3 — Auto-Detection

```
Niti reads the first message
and picks the right lens automatically.

"Should I expand..."     → Decision mode
"My partner isn't..."   → Therapist first
"What's happening in..." → Contrarian + Market
"Stress-test this"      → Devil's Advocate
"I'm stuck..."          → Therapist + First Principles
"Think through completely" → Full Chain

User never picks a mode.
Niti figures it out.
```

---

## Part 4 — Styles

Purple scheme throughout — distinct from ARYA (green) and KAAL (amber). Quick start chips slide right on hover. Textarea focuses purple on selection.

---

## Part 5 — Thinking Journal Entry

At the end of every session, user can save a journal entry. Niti writes it in second person — not a transcript, a narrative:

```
WHAT YOU CAME IN WITH:
You were deciding whether to open a second
clinic. The doctor was the catalyst but
the decision had been building for a year...

WHAT YOU FIGURED OUT:
The audit isn't the real constraint.
The question underneath is whether you
trust the partnership enough to build
something long-term with this person...

THE ONE THING TO CARRY FORWARD:
Don't decide about the clinic.
Decide about the doctor.

— Niti, June 15, 2026
```
