```
You're driving. An idea comes.
You're between patients. A task surfaces.
You're lying in bed. Something you 
don't want to forget tomorrow.

Old way:
  Try to remember → forget
  Type on phone → dangerous/inconvenient
  WhatsApp yourself → lost in chat

ARYA way:
  Tap Notes chip
  Tap Record
  Speak naturally
  Done in 10 seconds
```

### Moment 2 — ARYA Captures Everything

```
You speak for 30 seconds in Malayalam:
"ഡോ. എഡ്വിന വരുന്നു നാളെ.
 Extension decision എടുക്കണം.
 NABH documents Friday deadline.
 Bed 7 cultures follow up."

ARYA does three things automatically:

1. TRANSCRIBES:
   Full text in Malayalam ✅
   Exactly what you said ✅

2. SUMMARISES:
   • Dr. Edwina joining tomorrow
   • Extension decision needed
   • NABH documents due Friday
   • Bed 7 cultures — follow up
   
   In your language ✅
   Bullet points ✅
   Under 10 words each ✅

3. EXTRACTS:
   Tasks: Extension decision, NABH docs
   People: Dr. Edwina
   Deadlines: Friday (NABH), tomorrow (Edwina)
```

## What Notes Is For — By User Type

### Doctor / Professional
```
Between consultations:
  "Patient in bed 4 — repeat CBC tomorrow"
  "Call cardiology about the referral"
  "Article idea — POCUS in rural setting"

On rounds:
  "Ward 3 discharge needs family counseling"
  
After a hard case:
  "Today's case — need to process this"
  ARYA keeps it for Sunday Review
```

### Student
```
In class:
  "Remember to ask prof about
   chapter 7 — wasn't clear"
  
Studying:
  "This concept confuses me —
   come back to it"
  
Before exam:
  "Topics I'm weak on"
```

### Homemaker
```
At market:
  "Need to buy — atta, dal, coconut oil"
  
During day:
  "School fee due this Friday"
  "Amma's medicine running out"
  
Random thought:
  "I want to learn something new —
   think about what"
```

### Business Person

```
   regional languages, B2B"
  
After call:
```

### Anyone

```
Anytime:

```

## What Notes Is NOT

```
Not a journal (Goals/Review is for that)
Not a long document (Chat is for that)
Not a to-do list (Goals is for that)

Notes is the space between thought and action.
The 10 seconds that saves the idea.
The voice that becomes the task.
```
