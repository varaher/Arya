## What You're Actually Asking

There are two very different things this could mean:

```
Interpretation A:
ARYA generates a story (text) via Drishya,
and that story is then converted into
an actual video — moving images, scenes,
characters — automatically.

Interpretation B:
Drishya generates a story (text) and
displays it with rich visual accompaniment —
animated backgrounds, ambient visuals,
mood-matched imagery — while the text
streams and audio narrates it.
Not a "video" in the traditional sense,
but a deeply immersive reading experience.
```

These are very different in terms of technical complexity, cost, and what's actually possible today. Let me be honest about both.

---

## Interpretation A — Full AI Video Generation

```
Current state of the technology (June 2026):

The best video generation models available
via API right now:

→ Runway ML Gen-3 Alpha
→ Pika Labs API
→ Kling AI
→ Google Veo 2 (limited API access)
→ Sora (OpenAI — still very limited API)

What they CAN do today:
  Generate 5–10 second video clips from
  a text prompt. Atmospheric, abstract,
  beautiful. "A lamp glowing in a dark
  room, ink spreading on water, a forest
  at dusk."

What they CANNOT do reliably yet:
  → Consistent characters across clips
    (your main character looks different
    in every scene — this is still the
    biggest unsolved problem in AI video)
  → Long-form narrative (10+ scenes
    that tell a coherent story)
  → Indian faces, Indian settings with
    cultural accuracy — these models are
    heavily Western-biased in their
    training data
  → Dialogue or lip-sync within generated
    video (technically possible but
    unreliable and expensive)

Cost per Drishya story if you did this:
  Each story = ~8–12 scenes
  Each scene = 5–10 sec clip generation
  Cost per clip: $0.05–$0.50 depending
  on model and quality
  Cost per story: $0.40–$6.00 (₹35–₹520)

  At Elite plan (₹999/month), even 10
  stories per month at the high end =
  ₹5,200 in video generation costs alone.
  That's 5x your entire Elite subscription
  revenue for just that one user's stories.
```

**Honest verdict on Interpretation A:**

```
Technically possible to BUILD today.
Economically unsustainable for a
subscription product at current API prices.
Quality still inconsistent — especially
for Indian characters and settings.

This is 12–18 months away from being
viable for a consumer product at ARYA's
price points.
```

## Interpretation B — Immersive Story Experience (Viable Now)

```
This IS buildable today, at near-zero
incremental cost per story, and would
make Drishya genuinely distinctive.

Here's what it would look like:

When a Drishya story streams in —

1. BACKGROUND: A looping, mood-matched
   animated background appears behind
   the text. Not a generated video —
   a pre-built, hand-crafted animation
   loop matched to the story's "world"
   (Night = deep indigo with slow
   moving stars / Film = cinematic
   amber light / Everyday = warm paper
   texture with gentle movement)

2. AMBIENT AUDIO: A soundscape plays
   underneath — not music, sound.
   Crickets for a night story. Rain on
   a roof. A distant train. The sounds
   of the world the story is set in.
   (Freesound.org has royalty-free
   Indian ambient audio)

3. VOICE: Sarvam TTS reads the story
   aloud as it streams — which you've
   already built. But now the pacing
   is matched to the visual rhythm.

4. TEXT CINEMATICS: Individual lines
   of the story fade in and out as
   they're narrated — not a chat bubble,
   but a cinematic caption style.
   Like subtitles on a film you're
   watching in your mind.

5. CHAPTER TRANSITIONS: Between story
   sections, a short animated ink-wash
   transition plays — the same motif
   ARYA uses everywhere.

The result feels like an interactive
audiobook crossed with a short film —
without generating a single frame of
AI video. Every user's story is unique
(the text is generated fresh) but the
visual experience is always beautiful
and consistent.
```
