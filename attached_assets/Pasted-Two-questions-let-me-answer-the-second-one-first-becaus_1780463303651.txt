## The Astrological Foundation

My recommendation — **three texts, layered:**

### Layer 1 — The Core
**Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**
by Maharishi Parashara

```
The foundation of all Vedic astrology.
Written 3,000+ years ago.
Every Jyotishi in India learns from this.

What it gives KAAL:
- The 27 Nakshatras and their qualities
- Dasha system (planetary periods)
- House meanings
- Planetary relationships
```

This is the **Mahabharata of astrology.** Everything else descends from it.

### Layer 2 — The Practical
**Phaladeepika**
by Mantreswara (14th century)

```
More accessible than Parashara.
Focused on real-life predictions.
Strong on timing — muhurta, transits.

What it gives KAAL:
- Daily timing windows
- Auspicious moments for actions
- Transit effects on the individual
```

### Layer 3 — The Human
**Saravali**
by Kalyanvarma

```
Deeply focused on personality
and life patterns — not just events.

What it gives KAAL:
- How each nakshatra shapes thinking
- How dasha periods feel from inside
- The psychological dimension of timing
```

---

## Why These Three Together

```
Parashara  →  The mathematics of time
Phaladeepika →  When to act
Saravali    →  How it feels

KAAL uses all three.
User feels none of the complexity.
They just feel: "ARYA knows my time."
```

## One More — For The Language

**Thirukkural** (Tiruvalluvar, 3rd century BCE)

```
Not strictly astrology.
But the most precise wisdom text 
ever written in any Indian language.

133 chapters. 1,330 couplets.
Each one a complete thought.

On timing specifically:
Kural 484:
"Whoever waits for the right moment
 and acts with full force —
 the world bends toward them."

ARYA never quotes this.
But ARYA thinks like this.
```
