```typescript
`Generate a short personal daily quote for ${userName}.

 1-2 lines. Inspirational but specific to them.`
```

Claude returns a string. ARYA wraps it in the card UI.

## The Problem With Current Quote Style

Looking at the actual quotes being generated:

```
"Varah, your clarity in welcoming diverse minds today
will shape not just the conference, but lasting connections."

"Varah, let your words today carve clarity and connection,
bridging knowledge and hearts like the steady pulse of insight."
```

These are **too long, too ornate, too generic-sounding** despite being personalised. They read like corporate motivational posters — not like a wise friend speaking to you.

---

## What Makes A Great ARYA Quote

```
Bad (current style):
  "Varah, let your words today carve clarity and connection,
   bridging knowledge and hearts like the steady pulse of insight."

  Problems:
  ✗ Too many metaphors stacked together
  ✗ "steady pulse of insight" — meaningless
  ✗ Sounds like it was written by AI
  ✗ Nobody talks like this

Good (what it should be):
  "Varah, you've been carrying a lot lately.
   Today — just one thing. Do that well."

  OR:

  "The conference isn't about what you present.
   It's about who you're present with. — ARYA"

  OR simply:

  "Rest is not a reward for finishing.
   It's what makes finishing possible."

  Why these work:
  ✓ Short — one idea, not three
  ✓ Specific — feels personal
  ✓ Sounds like a real person said it
  ✓ Makes you pause and think
```

## The Fix — Better Quote Generation Prompt

```typescript
// server/arya/morning-briefing.ts

const quotePrompt = `
Generate today's personal reflection for ${userName}.

RULES — follow exactly:
- Maximum 2 sentences. Shorter is better.
- One clear idea. Not three ideas strung together.
- No stacked metaphors. No "bridges of insight" type language.
- Sound like a wise friend, not a motivational poster.
- Reference their actual life if context is available:
  ${userContext}
- If no context — write something universally true
  but phrased simply and personally.

GOOD examples:
  "You've been thinking about this decision for days.
   Today, trust what you already know."

  "Rest is not a reward for finishing.
   It's what makes finishing possible."

  "One clear conversation today is worth
   ten half-finished ones."

BAD examples (never write like this):
  "Let your radiant spirit bridge the cosmos of connection"
  "Carve clarity through the steady pulse of insight"
  "Embrace the transformative journey of becoming"

${langInstruction}

Return ONLY the quote text.
No quotation marks. No attribution. Just the words.
`;
```

## The Display Pattern — What Could Be Better

Current display:

```
"Long quote here spanning
 multiple lines with lots
 of ornate language."

— ARYA · TODAY'S REFLECTION
```

Better display:

```
✦  TODAY'S REFLECTION

"Short, punchy quote.
 One strong idea."

— ARYA
```

Or even simpler — remove the quotes entirely:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  ✦                          │
│                             │
│  Short, punchy quote.       │
│  One strong idea.           │
│                             │
│  — ARYA · Today             │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

Removing the quotation marks makes it feel more like ARYA is speaking directly to you — not reading from a book.

```
                 "BAD examples" section tells Claude what to avoid
```
